Customer safety

Singularity SEO stores only what it needs to operate the SEO control plane.

Final legal review is required before public launch, but the product scope and privacy posture are intentionally narrow.

Data processed

Connection

Customer/site ID, site URL, hosted connection record and authenticated ownership mapping.

SEO operations

Managed page registry, setup scans, health snapshots, proposals, applied changes and rollback records.

Visibility

Search Console property, ranking domain, tracked keywords and measured search data when connected.

Product scope

Singularity SEO is not a full WordPress administration tool. It does not manage hosting, billing, themes, menus, page body content or unrelated WordPress data.

Can read

Status, managed pages, protected governance, technical health, visibility configuration, reports and change history.

Can propose

SEO titles, descriptions, social metadata, campaign SEO and batch plans for approval.

Can change

Approved controlled SEO metadata and approved restores to prior controlled state.

Search data

Google Search Console may connect before Google returns measurable rows. That is a valid pending state. Rank refreshes may consume one provider search per tracked keyword.

Standard WordPress launch plan: - Up to three tracked keywords - Server-side daily rank-search limits - No ranking, traffic, revenue or AI/search placement guarantees

Removal and support

Customers can disconnect the ChatGPT app, remove the WordPress plugin or request hosted data deletion through the published support channel. Do not send passwords, private keys or OAuth secrets in support requests.
